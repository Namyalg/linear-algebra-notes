u·v = 2·1 + 1·√3 ≈ 3.73 — sameness along every axis

Each axis pairs its own components: the x-parts agree by 2·1 = 2, the y-parts agree by 1·√3 ≈ 1.73, and the dot product is their sum. The dashed segment is the projection of v onto u — its length along u is exactly u·v / ‖u‖ ≈ 1.67.

Why it's a sum: each axis = one rectangle

The x-axis contributes the area of a 2×1 rectangle, the y-axis a 1×√3 rectangle. The dot product is the total area — "agreement" summed over all axes.
