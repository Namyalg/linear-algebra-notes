Same line, different lengths

u₁ = (2,1)/√5 fixed. Four different v₂'s. Each leftover is the projection of v₂ onto the perpendicular line p̂ = (−1,2)/√5 — so it always lands on that line; only its length varies.
The rule: v₂ = (v₂·u₁)u₁ + (v₂·p̂)p̂ (unique split). Gram–Schmidt subtracts the u₁-part; the leftover is exactly (v₂·p̂)·p̂ — a projection onto p̂. A projection onto a line always lands on that line.
u₁ (built axis) p̂ = the perpendicular line v₂ (incoming) u₁-part removed (drop) leftover = (v₂·p̂)·p̂
