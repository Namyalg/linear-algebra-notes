Triangle inequality: ‖u + v‖ ≤ ‖u‖ + ‖v‖

Case 1 — same direction: equality

Case 2 — v at an angle: strict inequality

Head-to-tail is how you always draw u+v. Equality holds only when u and v are parallel and point the same way. Any angle > 0 loses the perpendicular part, so the straight side (u+v) is shorter than walking u then v — that's the triangle.
