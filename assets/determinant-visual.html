What to notice

  • det ≠ 0 (blue area exists) ⟺ u, v are independent — not multiples of each other ⟺ they span all of R² ⟺ every vector is buildable from them.
  • det = 0 (area collapses) ⟺ u, v are collinear — one is a scalar multiple of the other. The parallelogram is a flat line. They span only a line, so they can't reach everything.
  • Sign = orientation: positive if v is counterclockwise from u, negative if clockwise. Only the magnitude is "area."
  • One condition, four hats: not-parallel ⟺ det ≠ 0 ⟺ unique solution for every w ⟺ basis.
